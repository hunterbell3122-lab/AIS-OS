# Voice

Raw samples from Q2, pasted verbatim. Match this register when drafting internal
or informal content. **Don't fake this voice on external content (LinkedIn,
client email) without showing Hunter a draft first** — Sample 2 below shows the
business register is a different, more clipped mode.

## Sample 1 — personal / casual (texting)

Short, fragmented, self-deprecating, emoji-punctuated. Fast thought-to-text with
minimal editing — lowercase drift ("i" not "I"), rhetorical questions, tangents.

```
Omg, typical government. Always late for everything. On their own time 🤣 what are you going to do with all your free time?

Lol the biggest joke is when i look at myself in the mirror 🙈 but I was up at 4:30! Back to the same.

I love fruit flavor, but not on my pizza. I'm normal. Not a savage. But good you'll have plenty to eat later.

What monk was this? Who captured him?

Idk, I screwed up my melatonin by over using it. I mean i dream but I don't remember. If i could be anyone? Idk maybe a professional athlete. Idk who, maybe a Fútbol player. Or a kicker in the NFL. I don't want to run the risk of injury. Maybe an actor, high level like, Keanu or Leo, Brad Pitt? What about you? That's a fun question.

Hey look at you! That would be fun 😁

Yeah I'll definitely need to run a full marathon before, just to ensure I can do it. The bike and swim are easy parts.

Omg 4 shots is insane. You're gonna be bouncing all over the bus.

Oh Doug, he's an odd one that is for sure. And now that you have all the free space on your phone, best just get the app
```

## Sample 2 — business / client-facing

Direct, no filler. Leads with the practical detail (lead time), states the
decision already made ("we picked out"), ends with a clear call to action and a
link. No pleasantries, no hedging.

```
Steve, I have attached quote for the chairs with the vinyl we picked out and the table with the stain we picked that matched. The tables have a 4 week lead time from order date.

To review the Quote Tommy's Restaurant - Chairs and Tables, Click here: https://review.aq-fes.com/quote/CGGuDgaK
```
