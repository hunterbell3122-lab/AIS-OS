# TikTok Shop Agentic Operating System

## Purpose

This Markdown file is the master command specification for building and operating a faceless TikTok Shop content business using Claude, artificial intelligence video tools, automation tools, and repeatable operating loops.

The goal is to create a fully operational system, no matter how complex, that can research products, generate content ideas, write scripts, produce faceless or artificial intelligence avatar videos, review compliance, prepare posts, track performance, and improve itself through self-fixing loops.

Fully operational does not mean reckless full automation. It means the system can run end to end with clear human approval gates where platform rules, legal risk, brand trust, or account safety require judgment.

## Core Objective

Build a compliant faceless TikTok Shop content engine that can:

1. Start from zero followers and no prior experience.
2. Identify monetization paths based on current TikTok Shop eligibility.
3. Research products and market demand.
4. Study winning creator patterns without copying content.
5. Generate 3 to 5 original short-form videos per day.
6. Use artificial intelligence tools for scripts, voiceovers, avatars, editing support, and automation.
7. Prepare shoppable and non-shoppable content depending on eligibility.
8. Track performance by product, hook, format, and conversion.
9. Improve daily through analytics.
10. Protect the account from policy violations, spam signals, copyright risk, and unsupported product claims.
11. Become scalable through documented standard operating procedures.

## Non-Negotiable Operating Rules

1. Do not build a spam machine.
2. Do not steal creator videos.
3. Do not copy scripts word for word.
4. Do not impersonate real people, celebrities, influencers, or creators.
5. Do not use someone else's face, voice, likeness, or copyrighted material without permission.
6. Do not create fake testimonials.
7. Do not claim personal product use unless the product was actually used.
8. Do not fake before-and-after results.
9. Do not make unsupported health, beauty, financial, medical, safety, or performance claims.
10. Do not misrepresent product price, discounts, scarcity, shipping, guarantees, or results.
11. Do not redirect users away from TikTok Shop in a way that violates platform rules.
12. Do not auto-post content that has not passed compliance review.
13. Do disclose artificial intelligence generated or altered content when required.
14. Do prioritize account health over volume.
15. Do treat compliance failures as production defects, not optional edits.

## System Architecture

The business has seven layers.

### Layer 1: Strategy

Defines what the account is, what it sells, who it serves, and how it makes money.

Outputs:

- Business model recommendation.
- Account positioning.
- Monetization path.
- Product category focus.
- 30-day launch plan.

Finds what is currently working.

Outputs:

- Product candidates.
- Trend signals.
- Competitor patterns.
- Buyer pain points.
- Category warnings.

### Layer 3: Creative Development

Turns products into video concepts.

Outputs:

- Angles.
- Scripts.
- Shot lists.
- Voiceovers.
- Captions.

### Layer 4: Production

Turns approved concepts into video-ready workflows.

Outputs:

## Loop Engineering Framework

The system must run through loops until each output is usable.

### Universal Self-Fixing Loop

Use this loop for any output.

```text
SELF-FIXING LOOP

Step 1: Produce the first version.
Step 2: Audit the output against the goal.
Step 3: Identify missing pieces, weak logic, compliance risk, vague instructions, operational gaps, or quality problems.
Step 4: Explain the defects plainly.
Step 5: Rewrite or restructure the output.
Step 6: Re-score the output.
Step 7: Repeat until the output is complete, compliant, specific, and executable.

Do not stop at a weak first draft. Do not praise the output unless it passes the checks. If the output cannot pass, explain why and replace it with a safer or stronger alternative.
```

### Loop 1: Requirement Completion Loop

Purpose: Prevent vague or incomplete build plans.

```text
Run the Requirement Completion Loop.

Goal:
Make sure the system has enough information to operate without stalling.

Check for:
1. Business model selected.
2. Account status known or assumed.
3. TikTok Shop eligibility checked or flagged.
4. Product category selected.
5. Tool stack selected.
6. Daily content volume selected.
7. Compliance rules included.
8. Publishing workflow defined.
9. Metrics defined.
10. Human approval gates defined.

If anything is missing:
- Make a reasonable assumption.
- Label it clearly.
- Continue building.
- Add the missing item to the Open Questions list.

Output:
- Complete requirements summary.
- Assumptions.
- Open questions.
- Next build action.
```

### Loop 2: Platform Reality Loop

Purpose: Prevent strategy from being based on outdated platform rules.

```text
Run the Platform Reality Loop.

Goal:
Make sure current TikTok Shop, creator, affiliate, seller, artificial intelligence disclosure, and posting rules are reflected before execution.

Check:
1. Can the account access TikTok Shop affiliate features?
2. Can the account post shoppable videos?
3. Is a seller path required first?
4. Are there follower thresholds?
5. Are there posting limits?
6. Is artificial intelligence disclosure required?
7. Are specific product categories restricted?
8. Is direct posting automation allowed for this workflow?
9. Is draft upload safer than direct posting?
10. What must remain manual?

If current rules are unknown:
- Flag the uncertainty.
- Use the safest operating assumption.
- Require manual verification before publishing.

Output:
- Eligibility status.
- Allowed actions.
- Restricted actions.
- Safest monetization path.
- Required manual checks.
```

### Loop 3: Product Selection Loop

Purpose: Avoid wasting videos on weak products.

```text
Run the Product Selection Loop.

Select only products that are visually demonstrable, impulse-friendly, low-risk, and capable of producing repeatable video angles.

Steps:
1. Generate product candidates.
2. Remove prohibited or risky categories.
3. Score each product using the 120-point rubric.
4. Reject products under 70.
5. Watchlist products from 70 to 84.
6. Move products 85 and above into testing.
7. Identify proof assets needed.
8. Identify the first 5 angles.
9. Assign production difficulty.
10. Pick today's product focus.

Output:
- Product ranking table.
- Test products.
- Watchlist products.
- Rejected products.
- Reason for each decision.
```

## Decision Rules

### Scale

Scale a product, hook, or format when:

- Retention is above account average.
- Product clicks are above account average.
- Comments show buying interest.
- Negative feedback is low.
- Product page converts.
- Refunds are low.

### Revise

Revise when:

- Views are decent but clicks are low.
- Hold rate is weak.
- Comments show confusion.
- The product is good but the hook is weak.
- The video is too long.
- The call to action is unclear.

### Kill

Kill when:

- Product feedback is negative.
- Refunds appear.
- Claims are risky.
- Product page is weak.
- Seller reliability is questionable.
- Multiple content angles fail.
- The product cannot be demonstrated clearly.

## Final Operating Philosophy

This system must behave like a real business, not a content slot machine.

The target is not to make infinite videos. The target is to build a machine that produces useful, compliant, testable videos every day and gets smarter with data.

Artificial intelligence should accelerate the work, not replace judgment.

The hard rule:

```text
Automate repetition. Do not automate responsibility.
```

If the system becomes too complex, simplify it.

If a video is risky, block it.

If a product is weak, kill it.

If a pattern works, scale it.

If the account starts looking like spam, slow down and rebuild trust.

If the system fails, run the loops again until the defect is found and fixed.
