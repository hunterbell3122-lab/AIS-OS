# Priorities — Q3 2026 (through end of October)

1. **Guide Studio: From Built to Revenue-Generating.** It's already built — the
   question isn't "can I build this?" It's "why isn't this printing money yet?"
   If Guide Studio isn't generating consistent Etsy revenue by October, that's a
   failure of distribution and positioning, not product.

2. **Make the Single-Bet Decision, in Writing, by August 1.** Guide Studio is the
   short-game cash flow play. The Kitchen Design Tool is the long-game
   domain-advantage exit vehicle. They don't share the same runway. Pick which one
   gets discretionary hours this quarter, write it down, commit — no perpetual
   deferral. Five ventures across four markets means none get enough energy to
   compound.

3. **Build a Distribution Asset That Serves Every Bet.** Products exist, no owned
   audience. Etsy is rented land. Begin building one owned distribution channel
   this quarter — LinkedIn personal brand, email list, or content engine targeting
   buyer personas. Compounds regardless of which bet wins Priority 2; it's the
   infrastructure every venture needs to scale without trading time for money.
